Amper

Chemistry-aware autocorrect for Google Docs.

Write formulas, Greek letters, reactions, and scientific notation naturally as you type.

h2so4 →becomes H₂SO₄

sigma →becomes σ

equi →becomes ⇌

N2 + 3H2 equi 2NH3 →becomes N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃

Amper stays off until you enable it. Document text is processed locally in your browser.
